Your information

Privacy, clearly stated.

This notice explains what information clicksable. receives when you use our website, request a Creator Blueprint or AI Visibility Audit, contact our team, or use the Creator Reward portal.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Who is responsible

Clicksable operates this website and handles information submitted through it. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@clicksable.com. Please tell us which form or account your request relates to. Do not email passwords or sensitive personal information.

Information you choose to provide

Depending on the service you use, we may receive:

  • Creator forms: your public handle, email address, platform, audience range, niche, revenue range, and the challenge you describe.
  • Business forms: company name, website domain, email address, industry, company size, target search topics, and the visibility challenge you describe.
  • Project enquiries: the information you submit when you contact us or ask to discuss a project.
  • Creator Reward portal: account and profile information, creator submissions, campaign activity, and messages sent through portal features.

Please share only information relevant to your request. Do not include confidential, sensitive, or regulated information in free-text fields.

How we use information

  • To receive and respond to your enquiry.
  • To create the personalized brief or audit you requested.
  • To operate the Creator Reward portal and provide its features.
  • To protect, troubleshoot, and improve the website and services.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations and handle valid data requests.

Where form information goes

Website form submissions are sent to our Supabase database in the leads table. We use a Cloudflare Worker to request an AI-generated report from Google Gemini. The profile details needed to prepare a report are included in that request; the email field is not included in the report prompt. Supabase, Cloudflare, Google, and the hosting provider process information as service providers under their own terms and applicable safeguards. The Creator Reward portal uses Supabase for account authentication and portal data.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when needed to operate the service, protect people or the service, or comply with a lawful request.

Storage, retention, and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, provide the requested service, maintain the portal, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention can vary by record and service provider. You can ask us to delete information associated with your enquiry or account; some records may need to be retained where the law requires it.

We use service providers and technical safeguards intended to protect information. Internet transmission and storage cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and requests

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact hello@clicksable.com and describe your request. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

Children and external links

Our services are intended for businesses and creators and are not directed to children. The website may link to services operated by others; their privacy practices are governed by their own notices.

Updates to this notice

We may update this notice as our website or data practices change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Questions about your information?
