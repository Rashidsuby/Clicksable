## Comparison checklist

- [ ] Brand and registered legal name are distinguished and spelled consistently.
- [ ] Website URL resolves to `https://clicksable.com/`.
- [ ] Email and any phone number match the business-owned contact details.
- [ ] Each address is accurate, current, and correctly labeled as a head office or other location.
- [ ] Service descriptions match the live Creator and Business pages.
- [ ] Hours, service area, and location status are current where those fields exist.
- [ ] Duplicate, obsolete, or incorrectly attributed profiles are noted for correction.
- [ ] Changes are made only through an account the business controls, then rechecked publicly.

## Required inputs to finish the comparison

Provide the official profile URLs (or identify which profiles do not exist) and confirm the registered legal name, any public phone number, and which addresses are genuine public office locations. The public website alone cannot establish account ownership or verify these facts.
