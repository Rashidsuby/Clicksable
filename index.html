AI SEARCH OPTIMIZATION SERVICES

Questions about GEO and AI visibility

Clear answers about the service, measurement, platforms, and what to expect.

What are AI search optimization services?

They help businesses improve how clearly their information is represented in AI-assisted search and answer experiences. Work can include buyer-question research, SEO and content reviews, authority improvements, and repeated visibility checks.

What does a GEO audit include?

We agree the buyer questions and platforms to review, inspect answers and cited sources, assess relevant SEO and content foundations, and prioritize opportunities for the engagement.

Does GEO replace traditional SEO?

No. GEO works alongside technical SEO, useful content, page experience, and credible authority signals. Priorities depend on your audience, market, and starting point.

Which platforms can be reviewed?

Potential surfaces include Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini, Claude, Meta AI, and Grok. The set is agreed around your audience and engagement scope.

How do you measure AI visibility?

We record the prompt, platform, date, market, answer presence, brand mentions, citations, competitors, and source URLs. Repeating agreed checks compares observations over time; it is not a universal score or guarantee.

Can you guarantee that AI will recommend us?

No. Search and AI platforms control what they show, and answers can vary by query, location, and time. We strengthen the information available and report what we observe.

How much does GEO cost?

Pricing depends on research depth, platform coverage, content needs, and implementation scope. Your proposal sets out the agreed deliverables, timing, and price before work begins.

What is included in a proposal?

The proposal outlines goals, selected platforms and questions, audit and implementation scope, deliverables, reporting cadence, timing, and price.
